# Materials Availability Note

This study did not generate new biological materials, cell lines, organisms, plasmids, antibodies, or custom reagents.

The eggshell materials used for the analyses were obtained as described in the manuscript Materials and Methods. No material transfer agreement applies to the data and code archive deposited here.

Residual physical eggshell specimens, if available after the analyses, are subject to institutional storage, biosafety, and sample-preservation constraints. Requests for reasonable scientific reuse should be directed to the corresponding author named in the manuscript.
