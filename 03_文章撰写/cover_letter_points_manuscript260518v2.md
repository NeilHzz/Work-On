Title: OVAL glycan state aligns with mammillary organization and local hatching resistance across three bird species

Use this file as a drafting scaffold for the submission cover letter.

Recommended opening

Dear Editor,

We submit our manuscript, "OVAL glycan state aligns with mammillary organization and local hatching resistance across three bird species," for consideration as a Research Article. The study addresses how chemically specific states on shared eggshell matrix proteins align with cross-species differences in eggshell structure and hatching-relevant mechanics.

Core novelty points

- The work anchors the comparison to a conserved egg-tooth interface and follows a continuous chain from mammillary organization to glycoprotein state, structure, and local hatching mechanics.
- The matrix-protein toolkit remained largely shared across chicken, duck, and pigeon, but OVAL showed an ordered glycan-state shift that tracked structural and mechanical divergence.
- Re-Glyco and electrostatic analyses identified a corresponding progression in Ca2+-relevant surface accessibility.
- Finite-element simulation recovered the same chicken-versus-duck/pigeon contrast at the mammillary interface under inside-out hatching-like loading.
- The study identifies OVAL glycan state as the most informative molecular layer linking a chicken-like eggshell state to mammillary organization and local hatching mechanics.

Why this is broadly relevant

- The manuscript moves the comparison from descriptive eggshell differences to a chemically interpretable mechanism on a shared matrix background.
- The findings connect glycoprotein state to a biologically relevant mechanical endpoint rather than stopping at proteomic or structural description.
- The framework should be extendable to other abundant eggshell matrix proteins and to broader comparative questions in mineralized tissues.

Suggested significance sentence

By linking glycan-state variation on a shared matrix protein to mammillary organization and hatching-relevant mechanics, this study provides a cross-scale mechanism for comparative eggshell divergence.
