## One-Sentence Message for Each Slide

1. **Title**  
   OVAL glycosylation links eggshell matrix chemistry to mineral architecture and local shell response.

2. **Broken from outside: food; broken from inside: life**  
   Eggshell quality requires balance between external protection and hatchability.

3. **From shell traits to mineralization events**  
   Conventional traits describe shell quality outcomes, but not the mineralization events that build this balance.

4. **Matrix-guided biomineralization**  
   Matrix proteins regulate eggshell biomineralization, especially during mammillary-layer initiation.

5. **Comparative design across poultry species**  
   Chicken, duck, and pigeon provide a comparative system to identify conserved mineralization mechanisms.

6. **Shared egg-tooth loading, different shell architecture**  
   Shared egg-tooth loading makes mammillary microstructure a comparable key variable.

7. **Conserved eggshell matrix protein toolkit**  
   Key eggshell matrix proteins are conserved across species.

8. **Difference may lie in protein modification**  
   Protein modification, especially N-glycosylation, may explain functional differences among conserved matrix proteins.

9. **Intact N-glycopeptide profiling**  
   Intact N-glycopeptide profiling quantifies glycoform-level variation beyond protein abundance.

10. **Chicken matrix proteins show lower glycosylation**  
    Chicken key matrix proteins show relatively low glycosylation compared with duck and pigeon.

11. **Why focus on OVAL?**  
    OVAL is the clearest candidate linking glycosylation difference to mineral initiation.

12. **OVAL glycan state changes Ca2+ accessibility**  
    Chicken OVAL glycans are spatially compact and less shielding.

13. **Exposed Ca2+ hotspots support mineral initiation**  
    More exposed Ca2+ hotspots may promote OVAL-mediated mineral nucleation.

14. **Mammillary architecture predicts inside-out response**  
    Mammillary architecture corresponds to standardized inside-out local shell response.

15. **Working model and acknowledgements**  
    OVAL glycan state connects matrix protein chemistry, Ca2+ accessibility, mammillary mineralization, and shell response.
